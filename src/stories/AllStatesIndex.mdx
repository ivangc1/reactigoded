# Catálogo AllStates

Matrices visuales completas para inspección en light/dark de cada
componente del DS. Cada `AllStates` muestra todas las combinaciones
relevantes de variants, sizes y estados.

## Ola 1 (beta.19)

Componentes simples sin estados ocultos tras interacción:

- **Button** — variants × appearances × disabled × loading × sizes
- **Input** — sizes × states (default/error/success) × disabled × placeholder
- **Checkbox** — variants × estados (unchecked/checked/indeterminate) × disabled
- **Switch** — variants × estados (off/on/indeterminate) × disabled
- **Tabs** — variants (default/pills/vertical) × selected positions
- **Progress** — variants × sizes × valores (0/50/100/indeterminate)
- **Card** — variants × appearances × bordered × elevated × glass × interactive
- **Badge** — variants × outline × pill × dot × sizes
- **Spinner** — variants × sizes
- **Skeleton** — shapes × sizes
- **Divider** — variants × dashed × con texto × vertical
- **Breadcrumb** — niveles 2/4/6 con ellipsis
- **Avatar** + AvatarGroup — sizes × image/initials × status × rounded
- **Chip** — variants × sizes × removable × disabled

Total Ola 1: 14 componentes × 2 temas = 28 snapshots Chromatic.

## Ola 2 (beta.20)

Pendiente — componentes interactivos y compounds que requieren `play()`
para abrir estados o decisiones de scope caso-por-caso:

- Alert, Radio, Rating, Slider, Stepper
- Tooltip, ThemeSwitch, Timeline, Toast, Pagination
- Accordion, Dropdown, Modal, Navbar, Sidebar, Table
- Input compound, Textarea, Select

Total target tras Ola 2: ~30 matrices.
